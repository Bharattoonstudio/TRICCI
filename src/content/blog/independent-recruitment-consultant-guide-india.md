# The Independent Recruitment Consultant's Guide to Earning More in India

You placed 12 candidates last year. You know your domain cold — whether that is fintech engineering, senior sales leadership, or supply chain management. You have a network that took years to build.

And yet, after your agency takes its cut, you are earning a fraction of what your work is worth.

This guide is for you.

## The Math That Should Anger You

Let us be direct about the numbers.

A typical placement fee in India for a senior role is 8–10% of CTC. On a ₹40 LPA hire, that is ₹3.2–4 lakhs.

If you are working through a traditional agency, you are likely earning 25–35% of that fee. So on a ₹3.5 lakh placement, you take home ₹87,500–₹1,22,500.

The agency keeps ₹2.27–2.62 lakhs. For what? Providing you with a job board login and a billing department.

If you placed 12 candidates at an average fee of ₹3 lakhs, you generated ₹36 lakhs for your agency. You earned ₹9–12.6 lakhs. The agency kept ₹23–27 lakhs.

That gap is your opportunity.

## Why Most Consultants Stay Stuck

### The Client Dependency Trap

Many consultants stay with agencies because the agency owns the client relationships. You do the work, but the employer contract is with the agency. If you leave, you leave the clients behind.

This is by design. It is how agencies retain talent despite paying poorly.

The solution is to build your own client relationships — which is easier than it sounds when you have a platform that handles the commercial infrastructure for you.

### The Compliance Excuse

"I can't go independent because I don't want to deal with GST, invoicing, and contracts."

This is a legitimate concern, not an excuse. The administrative overhead of running an independent practice is real. But it is also solvable. Platforms like TRICCI handle billing, compliance, and payment processing — so you can focus on what you are actually good at.

### The Volume Fallacy

Some consultants believe that working through a large agency gives them access to more roles. In practice, most agencies have 3–5 anchor clients that generate 80% of their revenue. You are competing with your colleagues for the same mandates.

An independent consultant with 10 focused client relationships will consistently outperform a consultant with access to 100 unfocused ones.

## Building a High-Value Independent Practice

### 1. Niche Down Ruthlessly

The most successful independent consultants in India are not generalists. They are the person that every fintech in Bengaluru calls when they need a VP of Engineering. Or the consultant who has placed 40 supply chain leaders across FMCG companies in the last five years.

Depth beats breadth. A niche gives you:

- Faster sourcing (you already know the candidates)
- Higher credibility with employers (you understand their world)
- Better candidate relationships (you are a trusted advisor, not a cold caller)
- Premium fees (specialists command more)

Pick a function, a sector, or a geography. Own it.

### 2. Build Candidate Relationships Before You Need Them

The best consultants are not reactive. They are not scrambling to find candidates when a mandate comes in. They have been building relationships for months or years.

This means:

- Regular check-ins with placed candidates (not just when you need something)
- Tracking career milestones — promotions, company changes, new skills
- Providing value without an agenda — sharing relevant articles, making introductions, giving honest career advice

When a mandate comes in that fits someone in your network, you can move in days, not weeks.

### 3. Qualify Mandates Aggressively

Not every role is worth taking. A poorly defined mandate with an unrealistic timeline and a hiring manager who has already rejected 20 candidates is a trap.

Before committing to a mandate, ask:

- Is the budget realistic for the experience level required?
- Does the hiring manager have authority to make an offer?
- What happened with the last 3 candidates they interviewed?
- What is the actual timeline to hire?

Saying no to bad mandates is how you protect your time for good ones.

### 4. Price Your Work Correctly

Independent consultants often underprice themselves because they are comparing their fees to what they earned at an agency — not to what the work is worth.

A senior hire that takes 3–4 weeks of focused work, drawing on years of domain expertise and a curated network, is worth 8–12% of CTC. Do not discount this because you are independent. If anything, your focused attention is worth more than an agency's fragmented effort.

### 5. Use Technology to Scale

The administrative work that used to require an agency — job posting, candidate tracking, offer management, billing — can now be handled by platforms built for independent consultants.
